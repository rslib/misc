starlight-theme-rslib

The Starlight theme for rslib project docs. This page shows the theme on a sample rgit page, in dark and light. Hover a heading to light its node on the lane.

The page

The lane on the left of the text works like git log --graph. The list on the right uses the same line and fills the dot for the part you are reading.

Colors

Each card shows dark mode on the left and light mode on the right. Verdigris is the only accent. Brass and oxide show only where they mean something.

Rules it follows

  • Body lines stay between 45 and 80 characters.
  • Body text is 16px or more, with a line-height of 1.5 or more.
  • One accent color, 1px lines and almost no shadows.
  • Code colors follow the dark and light switch.
  • Callouts get a thin side line and a faint tint, not a full box.
  • Text passes WCAG AA contrast in both modes.
  • No all-caps labels. Less motion when the system asks for it.
